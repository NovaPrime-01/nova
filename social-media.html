01 — Purpose

Presence with a reason

Each channel has a job: share projects, showcase creative work, communicate updates, connect with developers and creators, explore technology-related communities and support professional networking. No claims are made about popularity, engagement, follower counts or audience size — this page describes intent and destinations, not metrics.

03 — Content direction

What each channel is for

These are proposed content directions, not a record of posts already published: Instagram for visual work and experiments; YouTube for demonstrations, tutorials and process; Discord for discussion and collaboration. If the accounts' focus shifts, this page and the site's social links update from a single content file — the handle, description and destination can never drift apart.

04 — On the site

One block, seen in four places

Social links live in a single source and render into the hero quick links, the Social Presence section, the Contact "also on" line and the footer — consistent labels, consistent destinations, no duplicated blocks to keep in sync. Every external link opens in a new tab with correct rel attributes, is reachable by keyboard and shows a visible focus state.

05 — Safety & privacy

Respecting visitors

The portfolio does not embed third-party social widgets, does not load platform feeds, does not collect social credentials and avoids tracking and analytics entirely — the links are plain anchors, which keeps them fast and private. If an embed were ever introduced, its privacy and loading cost would have to justify itself first. Link verification status is stated honestly: verified destinations are labelled, unverified ones are shown as handles only.
